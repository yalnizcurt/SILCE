Element Key Answer What it Means

3. Evidence Convergent evidence across 3,056 reviews, 35-person survey, and 4 user interviews. Three independent data sources validating the same root cause.
4a. User Value Instant outfit versatility proof + biometric size calibration without leaving app. Eliminates sizing doubt and exchange anxiety.
4b. Business Value +2.7pp conversion lift = ₹83.8 Cr incremental monthly GMV with ₹0 discount spend. High-margin monetization of existing organic demand.
5. Why Now Myntra's fast-fashion and D2C catalog grew 40% YoY; cross-brand sizing variance is at all-time high. Solving decision certainty is essential to maintain market leadership.
ROOT CAUSE BREAKDOWN
👤 USER: Fit anxiety • Fear of return hassle • Outfit coordination doubt
📱 PLATFORM: Flat 2D size charts • Standalone product isolation
🎨 EXPERIENCE: Mannequin studio renders hide real drape on regular frames
🛡️ TRUST: Skepticism regarding synthetic fabric and true colors

Multiple Solutions brainstormed, “StyleProof” outscores all

myntra RICE Framework
⭐ StyleProof (Lookbook + FitTwin)

• Ambient pink pill on wishlist card triggering Wardrobe Match Canvas + Verified FitTwin UGC review.

• Ingests past 12-month orders to pair with owned jeans/shoes + 5'9" biometric photo.

• 1-Tap ‘Move to Bag’ with calibrated size selector.

Why chosen: Zero user prompt friction. Renders directly on existing wishlist surface; attacks both sizing & styling frictions simultaneously.
RICE Score: 8 × 9 × 8 / 6 = 96.0
💬 Conversational AI Stylist

• Chatbot interface where users type prompts like “How to style this suede jacket?”

• AI replies with styling suggestions and accessory links.

Why discarded: High interaction friction (<8% open rate in retail). Fashion browsing is visual, not text-driven.
RICE Score: 4 × 6 × 5 / 7 = 17.1
🎰 Gamified Outfit Spinner

• Interactive spin-the-wheel to unlock outfit combinations.

Why discarded: Gamification feels like a discount coupon gimmick in India and randomizes deliberate purchase intent.
RICE Score: 5 × 5 × 6 / 5 = 30.0

07 / 10 • SOLUTION BRAINSTORMING & RICE PRIORITIZATION

Multiple Solutions brainstormed, “StyleProof” outscores all

myntra RICE Framework
⭐ StyleProof (Lookbook + FitTwin) SELECTED WINNER

• Ambient pink pill on wishlist card triggering Wardrobe Match Canvas + Verified FitTwin UGC review.

• Ingests past 12-month orders to pair with owned jeans/shoes + 5'9" biometric photo.

• 1-Tap ‘Move to Bag’ with calibrated size selector.

Why chosen: Zero prompt friction. Embedded directly inside wishlist; removes both sizing and pairing anxiety simultaneously.
RICE Score: (8 × 9 × 8) ÷ 6 = 96.0
💬 Conversational AI Stylist DISCARDED

• Standalone chatbot interface where users type prompts like “How to style this jacket?”

• AI generates textual advice and product links.

Why discarded: High interaction friction (<8% open rate in fashion apps). Browsing is visual, not text-driven.
RICE Score: (4 × 6 × 5) ÷ 7 = 17.1
🎰 Gamified Outfit Spinner DISCARDED

• Interactive spin-the-wheel to unlock random outfit combinations.

• Coupon unlocks for complete outfit purchases.

Why discarded: Gamification feels like a discount coupon gimmick and undermines premium brand trust.
RICE Score: (5 × 5 × 6) ÷ 5 = 30.0
